Striking a Balance: An Interview with an AI Expert

This report explores the evolving relationship between human decision-making and artificial intelligence in professional communication environments. In an interview with Greg Sligh, Senior Solutions Architect for Amazon Web Services, we evaluate how organizations can balance AI-driven efficiency with human oversight. Particular attention is given to high-stakes situations where automated outputs may introduce bias, misinformation, or ethical concerns. The report proposes a practical framework for determining when human intervention remains necessary despite advances in AI capabilities.

My Model, Collective Consciousness: Lessons From the Past, On the Present, and For the Future

This paper proposes a contemporary journalism framework designed to address declining public trust, technological disruption, and changing audience expectations. Drawing on both traditional journalistic principles and emerging digital practices, the project introduces the concept of "Collective Consciousness," a model that emphasizes community engagement, multimedia storytelling, and reporter-driven audience relationships. Through analysis of local journalism and evolving media ecosystems, the paper explores how journalists can balance public service responsibilities with the demands of modern digital platforms. The project argues that sustainable journalism depends on maintaining credibility while adapting to new technologies and audience behaviors.
