Audio

I fell in love with audio in the spring of 2020. While recording with my friends was initially a creative outlet during the pandemic, my work quickly shifted to a deeper focus on mixing, production, and creating music for other artists once I entered college. In the summer of 2023, I began producing original music, engineering live shows, and managing full-scale event audio systems, working as an Audio-Visual Technician at Clark University in Worcester, MA, Visual Technologies in Syracuse, NY, and Image Production Services in Shrewsbury, MA. After graduating from Clark in the spring of 2025 with a minor in Music Technology, I now work as an engineer, producer, and composer for concerts, films, a video game, and ongoing collaborations with artists.
