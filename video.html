Video

I began exploring video in the spring of 2023, creating student films that taught me the fundamentals of storytelling, cinematography, and editing. Shortly after, I worked for the Liverpool, NY movie studio American High as a Production Assistant, coordinating departments and supporting on-set operations during filming. Later that summer, I joined them as a Development Intern, analyzing scripts and providing feedback. I now direct, shoot, and edit music videos and short films, while also serving as a sound mixer and recordist. In addition, I write multiple original shorts, features, and television pilots.
