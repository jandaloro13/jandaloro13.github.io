Social Media Analytics: Pure Tech

This social media analytics project evaluates how Pure Tech uses platform-specific content strategies to build audience reach, strengthen brand identity, and distribute different forms of creative and professional content. The project examines the distinct role of each channel, from Instagram's visually driven portfolio of creative work and YouTube's long-form video content to TikTok's short-form entertainment and LinkedIn's educational and professional thought leadership. SoundCloud extends the strategy into original music and creative collaborations, allowing the brand to showcase its audio production capabilities alongside its visual and communications work. Across these platforms, Pure Tech has built a combined audience of more than 2,200 followers and subscribers, generating over 500K views across Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, and SoundCloud. The analysis demonstrates how adapting content to the native strengths of each platform can expand reach while creating a cohesive digital presence across multiple creative disciplines.

The Successes & Failures of Meme Marketing: Investigating Cultural Literacy & Authentic Participation

Meme marketing is a modern, participatory form of digital communication that succeeds or fails based on two central variables: cultural literacy and authentic participation. In this paper, I define meme marketing as a shift from traditional top-down advertising to a user-driven ecosystem where brands must engage as co-creators rather than broadcasters. My framework introduces the “literacy gap” and “authenticity gap” to explain how brands either successfully integrate into internet culture or fail through misinterpretation and forced imitation. Through successful case studies like Wendy’s and McDonald’s, and the failed counterparts of DiGiorno and Pepsi, my paper demonstrates how audience control, timing, and cultural context determine outcomes more than intent. Above all else, successful meme marketing depends on adaptive authenticity, shared psychological ownership, and real-time cultural awareness, while failure results in reputational damage, ridicule, and long-term loss of credibility.

Integrated Marketing Campaign: Cloud.10

This integrated marketing communications campaign was developed for Cloud.10, a fictional wellness-focused brand positioned around portable aromatherapy and self-care. The campaign combines social media, email marketing, influencer partnerships, and content strategy to strengthen brand awareness among environmentally conscious millennial consumers. Research into industry trends and audience behavior informed a six-month communications plan designed to increase engagement and drive measurable business outcomes. The project demonstrates how cohesive messaging and visual identity contribute to premium brand positioning.

From Hollywood to Worcester: Reviving the Theater Experience – A Business Plan for Worcester’s Only Theater

This business development proposal addresses the challenges facing independent cinemas in an era dominated by streaming platforms and changing audience habits. Focusing on Cinema Worcester, the project outlines strategies for increasing attendance through community partnerships, targeted programming, and experiential event design. Recommendations include collaborations with local colleges, audience-specific programming initiatives, and internship opportunities that strengthen local engagement. The proposal positions the theater as a cultural gathering space rather than solely a venue for film exhibition.
