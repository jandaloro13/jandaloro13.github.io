Marketing

I began exploring marketing in the fall of 2021 as I entered college, applying creativity and strategy to content creation and audience engagement on social media. Early projects included managing campaigns for the student organization Mixing with Musicians and promoting my own work through Pure Tech Productions, my personal entity for sharing music, videos, and art. During the summers of 2022 and 2023, I served as a Program Assistant and Financial Advisor at CNY Works, creating content for multiple platforms to support a nationwide economic awareness campaign. I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Communication with a concentration in Public Relations, on track to graduate in the spring of 2026, while developing marketing strategies to grow audiences across Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, and YouTube.
