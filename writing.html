Writing

Writing has been a passion of mine for as long as I can remember. As a child, I wrote short stories, novel chapters, and poetry, developing skills that later extended into screenwriting during college. In the fall of 2021, I was a finalist in the Loring Holmes & Ruth Dodd Drama Contest, and in the fall of 2023, I wrote a short film produced for the 7 Day Film Festival, both at Clark University. I graduated with a Bachelor of Arts in the spring of 2025 with a minor in Creative Writing, and now focus on writing original shorts, feature-length projects, and television pilots.
