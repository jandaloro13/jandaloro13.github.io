About

I am a marketing and communications professional with experience in content strategy, audience engagement, project management, and multimedia communications.

My work focuses on developing communications strategies that align messaging, audience needs, and business objectives across digital platforms. My graduate work in public relations further strengthened my expertise in integrated marketing communications, digital strategy, crisis communications, and multi-channel campaign development.
