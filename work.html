Work

While attending high school, I formed Pure Tech Productions as a personal platform for digital communication and media production. What started as a creative outlet has evolved into a production entity where I not only work on films and music, but also engage with my audience and manage social media rollouts, reaching more than a million impressions across platforms.

In the summers of 2022 and 2023, I worked at CNY Works in Syracuse, NY as a Program Assistant and Social Media Manager for a state-funded youth employment program serving over 500 participants. My role was to connect the youth of Syracuse with beneficial summer jobs, as well as create social content for a national awareness campaign, deliver professional development presentations, and act as a communication bridge between state offices, employers, and students.

In April 2023, I worked with the Liverpool, NY movie studio American High as a Production Assistant, supporting on-set talent, managing equipment runs, and coordinating across departments to keep shooting days on schedule. While the role was focused on production, it strengthened my understanding of organized communication, clear task delegation, and alignment between different teams, all under time constraints. Later during my Fall 2023 sophomore year, I returned to the film studio as a Script Reader, reading over 40 feature-length scripts under an NDA and providing feedback on whether or not the film should go forward into production. This role sharpened my analytical writing, audience evaluation, and message clarity, preparing me for my current passion for public relations.

In August 2023, I joined Clark University as an Audio-Visual Technician, running sound, lighting, and video for campus events. In addition to hardware operation and equipment experience, I also expanded my client communication skills by interacting with faculty, guest performers, and student leaders to turn their plans into achieved goals. Throughout my time working at Clark, I constantly adapted to new situations, resolved issues without disrupting events, and maintained professional communication under pressure.

From May to August 2024, I worked for Visual Technologies in Syracuse, NY, building and operating live event systems for corporate and entertainment venues as an Audio-Visual Technician. Once again, my role required consistent client communication and involved situations where timing and safety were non-negotiable. This was my first experience in a professional setting with live event production, and I began to pursue it as a full-time employee in Massachusetts the following year.

In January 2025, I began working at Image Production Services in Shrewsbury, MA as an Audio-Visual Technician, assisting on large-scale conferences and concerts while continuing to learn professional production workflows. By March of 2025, I was hired full-time and further developed my ability to manage client needs, align technical operations with event objectives, and clearly communicate under pressure.

I currently freelance, integrating communication strategy alongside media production. In the summer of 2025, I worked as the Project Manager for the video game and short film Chasing Rabbits, leading a team of 10 developers and serving as the primary communication lead over the entire project timeline. Additionally, beginning in the fall of 2025, I expanded into public relations writing and internal documents for the startup production and creative consulting company Out to Space Studios.

Across every role, whether related to communications or media production, I treat strategy and execution as equal parts of the same system. My early work forced me to understand every part of the process, solving problems under pressure and delivering on clients’ needs. Later and larger opportunities have introduced heightened logistics and coordination, where precision matters as much as creative vision. These experiences have solidified my ability to manage complex projects with higher stakes and narrower margins for error, forming the environment in which I continue to pursue professionally.
