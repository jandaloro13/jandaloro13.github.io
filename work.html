In December 2020, I founded and began working as a Marketing Communications Manager for Pure Tech, a platform for digital communications, content creation, and media production. What began as a creative outlet has evolved into a multifaceted production entity where I oversee content strategy, production, and distribution across video, audio, graphic, and written media. Through the creation and promotion of more than 350 multimedia assets supporting over 10 original and client-focused campaigns, I have generated more than 1 million impressions, 500,000 views, and 2,000 followers across YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn. In addition to producing films and music, I manage audience engagement, social media communications, copywriting, graphic design, and branded content initiatives designed to strengthen brand visibility, audience growth, and campaign performance.

Beginning in April 2025, I have been freelancing at Out to Space Studios in Worcester, MA as a Project Manager. So far, I have managed two concurrent multimedia productions, coordinating project timelines, deliverables, and stakeholder communication across five functional teams to maintain alignment and production continuity. I led a 10-member cross-functional team through project planning and execution, overseeing workflows, schedules, task management systems, and team accountability from development through production. In addition to project leadership, I support client-facing communications, strategic planning, and operational documentation, helping creative projects move efficiently from concept to completion while ensuring stakeholders remain informed and aligned throughout the process.

I also delivered more than 40 presentations communicating employment opportunities, program requirements, and professional development resources, helping translate complex information into accessible guidance for diverse audiences while supporting recruitment, onboarding, and day-to-day program operations.
