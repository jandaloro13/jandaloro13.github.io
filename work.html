Work

While attending high school, I formed Pure Tech Productions as my personal outlet for music, film, and digital media. What started as a small recording setup evolved into a production entity that allows me to write, direct, and edit music videos and short films, engineer and mix tracks for other artists, and manage social media rollouts that have reached more than a million impressions across platforms.

In the summers of 2022 and 2023, I worked at CNY Works in Syracuse, NY as a Program Assistant and Social Media Manager for a state-funded youth employment program serving more than 500 participants. My role was to connect the youth of Syracuse with beneficial summer jobs, providing them with financial assistance and professional experience. I created social content for a national awareness campaign, delivered financial literacy presentations, and acted as a communication bridge between state offices, employers, and students.

In April of 2023, I worked with the Liverpool, NY movie studio American High as a Production Assistant. On set, I handled talent movement, equipment runs, and cross-department coordination to keep shooting days on schedule, sparking my passion for being a part of a professional production team. Later during my Fall 2023 sophomore year, I returned to the film studio as a Script Reader. My responsibility was to read over 40 feature-length scripts under an NDA, providing feedback on whether or not the film should go forward into production. Through this role, I was able to strengthen my analysis of creative work, improving my own work in the process.

In August of 2023, I joined Clark University as an Audio-Visual Technician, running sound, lighting, and video for campus events. Not only did this work give me more experience with production and its technology, but it also strengthened my technical troubleshooting and client communication skills, allowing me to see the concept, complexity, and completion of clients’ production needs.

From May to August of 2024, I worked for Visual Technologies in Syracuse, NY, building and operating live event systems for corporate and entertainment venues across central New York. I handled load-ins, front-of-house audio, LED walls, and multi-camera setups, where timing, safety, and precision were non-negotiable. This was my first experience in a professional setting with live event production, and I began to pursue it as a full-time employee in Massachusetts.

In January of 2025, I began interning at Image Production Services in Shrewsbury, MA, assisting on large-scale conferences and concerts while closely learning the professional-level production workflow. By March of 2025, I was hired full-time. I now handle live sound engineering, lighting design, video switching, and full event builds as an operator and client-facing Audio-Visual Technician.

I currently freelance, expanding into communication strategy alongside production. In the summer of 2025, I co-directed, wrote the script, and produced all audio for the video game demo Chasing Rabbits, leading a team of 10 developers and providing a 45 page game design document. Additionally, beginning in the fall of 2025, I began writing internal documents and public relation outreach articles for the startup production and creative consulting company Out to Space Studios.

Across every role, whether freelance or corporate, I treat technical execution and communication strategy as equal parts of the same system. My early work forced me to understand every part of the process, solving problems under pressure and delivering on clients’ needs. Larger productions introduced heightened logistics and coordination, where timing, safety, and precision mattered as much as creative intent. The scale increased, the stakes rose, and the margin for error narrowed, forming exactly the environment I wanted to grow in and the one I plan to continue my career in.
