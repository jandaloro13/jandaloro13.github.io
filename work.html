Work

Beginning in high school, I formed Pure Tech Productions as my personal outlet for music, film, and digital media. What started as a small recording setup evolved into a production entity that allows me to write, direct, and edit music videos and short films, engineer and mix tracks for other artists, and manage social rollouts that have reached more than a million impressions across platforms.

I began my undergraduate career at Clark University in Worcester, MA in August of 2021, majoring in Media, Culture, and the Arts and minoring in Music Technology and Creative Writing. During my time as an undergraduate, I started to focus more on the behind-the-scenes of production, communication, and business in general. I have taken classes on media across audio, video, art, and writing, utilizing them to expand my expertise and experience. During my time at Clark, I exhibited photography and videography in student art shows, published art in student magazines, and was a finalist in a student writing contest. Throughout my entire time as an undergraduate, I have achieved Dean’s List and been a recipient of an achievement scholarship. In May of 2025, I graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Arts degree.

In the summers of 2022 and 2023, I worked at >CNY Works in Syracuse, NY as a Program Assistant and Financial Advisor for a state-funded youth employment program serving more than 500 participants. My role was to connect the youth of Syracuse with beneficial summer jobs, providing them with financial assistance and professional experience. I created social content for a national awareness campaign, delivered financial literacy presentations, and acted as a communication bridge between state offices, employers, and students.

In April of 2023, I worked with the Liverpool, NY movie studio American High as a Production Assistant and background actor. On set, I handled talent movement, equipment runs, and cross-department coordination to keep shooting days on schedule. This position sparked my passion for being a part of a professional production team, collectively tackling problems and accomplishing goals.

In August of 2023, I joined Clark University as an Audio-Visual Technician, running sound, lighting, and video for campus events. Not only did this work give me more experience with production and its technology, but it also strengthened my technical troubleshooting and client communication skills, allowing me to see the concept, complexity, and completion of clients’ production needs.
