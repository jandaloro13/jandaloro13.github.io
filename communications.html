Communications

I began exploring public relations and communications in the fall of 2021 as I entered college, applying creativity and strategy to content creation and audience engagement on social media. Early projects included managing campaigns as the Social Media Director for the student organization Mixing with Musicians and promoting my own work through Pure Tech Productions, my personal entity for media production. During the summers of 2022 and 2023, I served as a Program Assistant and Social Media Manager at CNY Works, creating content for multiple platforms to support a nationwide economic awareness campaign. I have also developed internal communications for two projects, including and a game design document for the video game demo Chasing Rabbits in the spring of 2025 and a breakdown of a colleague's startup production company Out to Space Studios in the fall of 2025. I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Communication with a concentration in Public Relations and am on track to graduate in the spring of 2026.
