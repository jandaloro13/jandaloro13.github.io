Education

I began my undergraduate career majoring in Media, Culture, and the Arts and minoring in Music Technology and Creative Writing at Clark University in Worcester, MA in August of 2021. During my time as an undergraduate, I started to focus more on the behind-the-scenes of production, communication, and business in general. I have taken classes on media across audio, video, art, and writing, utilizing them to expand my expertise and experience. During my time at Clark, I exhibited photography and videography in student art shows, published art in student magazines, and was a finalist in a student writing contest. Throughout my entire time as an undergraduate, I have achieved Dean’s List and been a recipient of an achievement scholarship. In May of 2025, I graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Arts degree.

In August of 2025, I began my Master of Science in Communication degree with a concentration in Public Relations at Clark University. My Master’s program has allowed me to build on the production experience I already have while formalizing the strategy side of messaging, audience analysis, and media planning. My goal now is to merge both production and communication into work that makes creative projects efficient and ready for an audience. I am currently on track to finish my degree in the spring of 2026.
