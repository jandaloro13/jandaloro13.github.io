Education

In May 2025, I graduated Magna Cum Laude with a Bachelor of Arts in Media, Culture, and the Arts and a double minor in Music Technology and Creative Writing from Clark University. During my time as an undergraduate, I studied the behind-the-scenes of communication and production, taking classes on writing, video, and audio to expand my expertise and complement my professional experience. As a student, I exhibited photography and videography in student art shows, published art in student magazines, and was a finalist in a student writing contest while earning Dean’s List recognition and receiving an academic achievement scholarship.

In May 2026, I finished my Master of Science in Communication degree with a concentration in Public Relations at Clark University, graduating with a 3.9 GPA. My graduate coursework included Integrated Marketing, Multi-Channel Communications, Public Relations, Crisis Communications, and Project Management. For my Master's Thesis, I produced an analytic and reflective Capstone Project that examines how companies can utilize internet comedy to successfully connect with their target audience online. My graduate program has allowed me to combine production expertise with a formal communication approach, preparing me to deliver creative projects that are both strategic and executed effectively.
